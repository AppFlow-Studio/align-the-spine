## Evidence required before changing a disposition

- A dated operational statement from the practice that distinguishes routine in-office catchment from eligible accident/PIP home-visit consideration.
- Current map route context with source, date, endpoints, and an explicit variability note; never promise travel time.
- Search Console/Keyword Planner or first-party query evidence where available.
- Aggregated patient-origin evidence only; no names, addresses, diagnoses, accident narratives, or other PHI.
- At least three materially unique patient-helpful modules supported by official local sources.
- A similarity/cannibalization review against the hub and every approved city page.
- Clinician/compliance approval for medical/PIP copy and explicit city-level home-visit verification before mentioning it.
