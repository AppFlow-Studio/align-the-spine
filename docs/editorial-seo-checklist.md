# Editorial SEO, GEO, and compliance checklist

## Every public item

- [ ] One clear intent; unique title, H1, slug, SEO title, and meta description.
- [ ] Concise direct answer beneath the opening context.
- [ ] Entity/geography is unambiguous: Align the Spine Chiropractic, Dr. Abe Nasser, one Deerfield Beach office, Florida.
- [ ] Original, patient-helpful content; no keyword substitution, arbitrary length target, hidden stuffing, or copied passage.
- [ ] Logical H2–H4 hierarchy; no editor-created H1.
- [ ] Visible author, meaningful update date, and clinician reviewer/date where required.
- [ ] Objective medical/legal/insurance/statistical claims bind to verified sources with date/scope.
- [ ] Emergency/red-flag guidance appears when relevant and does not position chiropractic as emergency care.
- [ ] No diagnosis, outcome, coverage, payment, timing, price, availability, or home-visit promise.
- [ ] Natural internal links only to published/indexable destinations.
- [ ] Approved representative image with dimensions, focal treatment, and useful alt (or explicit decorative choice).
- [ ] Canonical points to the approved production domain; no override unless admin-reviewed.
- [ ] Structured data matches visible content; no rating/review/FAQ/HowTo schema added as a tactic.
- [ ] Rendered accessibility preview passes keyboard, headings, focus, contrast, reflow, images, and link-purpose checks.

## Blog-specific

- [ ] Direct answer/key takeaways are accurate and not overconfident.
- [ ] Sources section identifies primary sources where possible.
- [ ] Reading time is calculated from body blocks.
- [ ] Related services/conditions/areas/articles are editorially chosen.
- [ ] Clinical guidance has a distinct clinician reviewer, review date, and no substantive post-review edit.

## Service-area-specific hard gate

- [ ] Operational evidence proves the area is legitimately served.
- [ ] Relationship is office city, verified adjacent in-office catchment, or verified extended accident consideration—not a fake location.
- [ ] The page clearly states the office is in Deerfield Beach.
- [ ] Home visits are mentioned only with city-level verification and limited eligible car-accident/PIP wording.
- [ ] At least three materially unique modules/proof points answer a local patient need.
- [ ] Local statistics use current official sources with geography, period, definition, date, and restrained interpretation.
- [ ] Similarity score is ≤40 and uniqueness score is ≥70.
- [ ] No fake address, pin, staff, testimonial, directions, patient count, review, NAP, or “located in” claim.
- [ ] No promised drive time, availability, insurance/PIP coverage, price, or home visit.
- [ ] Cannibalization review supports a standalone page instead of the hub.

## Florida PIP language

Use general, sourced wording: Florida PIP rules can involve time-sensitive requirements, including an initial-care timing rule. Coverage and eligibility depend on the policy and circumstances. Direct claim-specific questions to the insurer or a qualified professional. Never state that benefits automatically vanish, care is free, payment is guaranteed, treatment protects a legal case, or every accident patient qualifies.

## Index decision

Only published, past-dated, passing-gate, indexable content enters public queries, metadata, sitemap, feed, schema, and internal discovery. Preview is always noindex/nofollow. Tags remain noindex until a usefulness/uniqueness review deliberately promotes them.
